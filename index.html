Game composer / Music designer

Music that gives worlds a pulse.

Jim Lau is a game composer who has worked on TGA award-winning projects. He is a level music designer, jazz pianist, and educator.

A graduate of Hamburg Konservatorium, he now blends jazz, classical, and hybrid game music, with design experience spanning performance, level flow, and combat. His main project is Wuthering Waves.
